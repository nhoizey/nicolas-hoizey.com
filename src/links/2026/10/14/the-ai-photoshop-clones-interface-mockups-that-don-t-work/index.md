> Artcraft took screen shots and a list of functions and got Claude to generate code based on those. The functions exist in the app, but a lot of them don’t work. You end up with something that looks like Photoshop, and it works a bit, until you try anything beyond what Microsoft Paint does. Or maybe curves. Or editing a 10,000 by 10,000 pixel image. Or cut-and-pasting without the app hanging.
