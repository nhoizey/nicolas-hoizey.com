> You spend a poignant hour listening to the synthesised voice of Donald Trump read the Grokipedia page for 9/11. Somewhat skeptical of the source, you start a deep research chatbot to corroborate the facts. Your research chatbot cannot find any information for November 9th and writes its conclusion to dev/null. As you reach the memorial, chatbot Trump reaches the climax of his audio tour by blaming Biden and directs you to exit through the gift shop where they’re selling two-for-one golden Twin Tower merch. You ask the gift shop chatbot why one of the towers looks like Orthanc at Isengard. The chatbot explains a translation error when placing the order with its Chinese counterpart.

Both hilarious and terrifying…
