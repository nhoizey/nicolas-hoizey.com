> We finally can style a select. The bigger change is what an option can hold. Until now, an option was one line of plain text, so everything a person needed to decide had to fit in a label or be left out. <mark>Now an option can carry the thing people actually decide with: a preview, a number, a job title, a warning</mark>. They stop reading and working things out, and start recognising.
